Welcome to our Random Music Generator!

Welcome to our unique and innovative music generator! Our application, built entirely with pure JavaScript and utilizing the Web Audio API, offers you two distinct music options to choose from: "eight bits" for a more upbeat and energetic sound, and "sine" for a softer and more ambient sound. The music is generated randomly in real-time, creating unique melodies and rhythms every time you hit play. Discover the endless possibilities of our randomly generated music and let the beats transport you!
